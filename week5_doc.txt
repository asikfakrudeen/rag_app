At a glance

What this week is about
Until now you fixed whatever problems you happened to notice. That misses the ones you didn’t see. This week you become systematic: read a real batch of the app’s answers, honestly note what went wrong in each, and turn that into a ranked list of the top problems worth fixing.
Why it matters

What you’ll learn
What a complete “trace” is — enough detail to replay any answer later
Taking a fair, random sample instead of cherry-picking nice examples
Reading each failure and writing one honest sentence about what went wrong — before deciding on categories
Grouping those notes into a handful of named problem types
Ranking the problem types by how often they happen and how much they hurt
Picking the one problem to attack next, and writing down what you expect to happen
Topics covered
The exact concepts to study.
